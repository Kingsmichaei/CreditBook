The short version: we collect what we need to run your account and your business records. We don't sell your data, and the records you enter about your customers belong to you.

1. Who we are

CreditBook is a business record-keeping service operated by Omkcode ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit our website or use the CreditBook application (the "Service").

For questions about this policy, contact us at kingsmichael.x@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number, password (stored only in hashed form) and business name.
  • Business records: the sales, expenses, income, debts, customer and staff records you enter into the Service.
  • Communications: messages you send us, for example support requests.

Payment information

Subscription payments are processed by our payment provider, Nomba. We do not receive or store your full card number or security code. We keep a payment token, the card type and a masked card number so that we can bill your subscription and show you which card is on file.

Information collected automatically

  • Log and device data: IP address, browser type, pages visited and timestamps, which our servers record as part of normal operation.
  • Cookies and local storage: we use essential cookies to keep you signed in and protect forms against forgery, and your browser's local storage to remember your light or dark theme preference. We do not use advertising cookies.

3. How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • create and manage your account and workspace, and authenticate you and your staff;
  • provide the Service's features, including dashboards, reports and exports;
  • process subscription payments and manage your billing status;
  • send service emails such as trial reminders, payment receipts, failed-payment notices and account-security messages;
  • respond to support requests;
  • keep the Service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems; and
  • comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use your business records for advertising.

4. Your customers' information

When you record information about your own customers or staff, such as names, phone numbers and amounts owed, you decide what is collected and why. For that information you are responsible for having a lawful basis to collect it and for telling the people concerned where required. We process it only on your instructions, to provide the Service to you.

WhatsApp reminders are sent from your own WhatsApp account. CreditBook opens a chat with a pre-filled message; we do not send messages on your behalf and do not receive the content of your WhatsApp conversations.

5. How we share information

We share personal information only with:

  • Service providers who help us run the Service, such as our hosting provider, email delivery provider and payment processor (Nomba). They may use the information only to provide their services to us.
  • People in your workspace: staff you add can see the business records their role allows.
  • Authorities where we are required to by law, or where necessary to protect the rights, property or safety of our users or others.
  • A successor business if CreditBook is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case we will notify you.

6. How long we keep information

We keep your account and business records for as long as your account exists. If your subscription lapses, your records are retained while your workspace is read-only or paused, so that you can pick up where you left off.

When you delete your account from your profile page, we permanently delete your account and workspace data. We may keep limited billing records for as long as required for tax, accounting or legal purposes.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, separation of each business's records, and email confirmation for sensitive actions such as account deletion. No online service can be perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and tell us straight away if you suspect unauthorised access.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct inaccurate information;
  • have your information deleted;
  • object to or restrict certain processing;
  • receive a copy of your information in a portable format; and
  • complain to a data protection authority, such as the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

You can update most account details and delete your account yourself from your profile page. For anything else, email kingsmichael.x@gmail.com. If you are a customer of a business that uses CreditBook, please contact that business first, as they control your information.

9. Children

CreditBook is intended for businesses and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above, and for significant changes we will notify account owners by email or in the app before the changes take effect.
