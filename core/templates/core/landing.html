FAQ

Common questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Get in touch and we'll help.

Do I need a card to start the free trial?

No. Create an account and you get 14 days of full access straight away. You only add a card when you decide to subscribe.

What happens when my trial ends?

If you haven't subscribed, your workspace becomes read-only: you can still see all your records but can't add new ones. After seven more days the workspace is paused until you subscribe. Your data is kept either way.

How do WhatsApp reminders work?

From a debt or customer record, tap the WhatsApp button. CreditBook opens a chat with that customer and a pre-filled message showing what they owe. You review it and send it from your own WhatsApp.

Can my staff use CreditBook?

Yes. You can create logins for staff members. Owners and managers can manage staff and billing; other staff can record day-to-day activity.

Who can see my business data?

Only you and the people you add to your workspace. Each business's records are kept separate. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. You can permanently delete your account and workspace from your profile page at any time. We confirm the request by email first.
