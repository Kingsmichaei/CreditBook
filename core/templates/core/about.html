Why CreditBook exists

In shops, markets and service businesses, a lot of trade happens on credit: a regular customer takes goods today and pays at the end of the month. It's good for business, but it's usually tracked in a notebook or from memory, and that's where money quietly goes missing.

CreditBook replaces the notebook with a simple ledger that lives on your phone and computer. Record a sale or a debt in seconds, see what's overdue at a glance, and send a polite WhatsApp reminder without the awkward conversation.

What we care about

Simplicity

If it takes more than a few taps to record a sale, it won't get recorded. We keep every workflow short.

Trustworthy records

Your numbers need to be right, private and there when you need them, on any device.

Fair pricing

One plan with every feature, and a free trial long enough to know whether it works for you.
